# Product marketing context: Crack The Campus

Document version: 1 (24 Nov 2026). Drafted from ctc-light-mockups.html and ctc-wireframes.html; review and correct.

## Product
Placement preparation platform for engineering students in India. Students learn and practise on the web, take proctored mock tests in a desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux) and get a CTC Score from 0 to 10 that shows placement readiness. Colleges get an admin suite: proctored internal exams, placement training (CRT) tracks, readiness analytics and a curriculum vault with an AI tutor.

## Audiences (ICP)
1. **Engineering students**, 2nd year to final year, mostly tier-2/3 colleges, preparing for mass recruiters (Infosys, Wipro, Capgemini, HCL, LTIMindtree) and some product companies (Oracle, Microsoft). Primary action: start free.
2. **Placement officers (TPOs), HODs and principals.** They own placement results and need early readiness signals and secure internal exams. Primary action: book a 20-minute demo.

## Positioning
Free sites give students questions. Crack The Campus adds company-pattern mocks, real proctored test conditions and one readiness score that tracks progress over time.

## Competitors
FACE Prep, PrepInsta, IndiaBix (free practice), plus college-run CRT vendors.

## Pricing (real)
Free ₹0 forever. Pro ₹3,499 for 6 months / ₹5,499 for 12. Pro + Placement ₹7,499 for 6 months / ₹9,999 for 12. Colleges: per-student annual licence, custom quote.

## Voice
A sharp senior who has been through placements: plain words, sentence case, real product, no hype. “The exam hall, but on your side.” Avoid: guarantees of jobs, “unlock”, “seamless”, “X, not Y” reveals.

## Customer language
“placement drive”, “online test”, “mock”, “pattern”, “sectional timer”, “cleared the first round”, “TPO”, “CRT”.

## Open items
Verified stats, consented testimonials, contest dates, phone/WhatsApp, support hours and response time are still sample data (see content/dummy.ts).
